# 🏥 Royal Park Clinic — Official Website

A mobile-first, responsive website built for **Royal Park Clinic**, a 5-star general medical clinic located in Ruwa, Zimbabwe.

🌐 **Live site:** [royal-clinic-37fb48.netlify.app](https://royal-clinic-37fb48.netlify.app)

---

## 📋 About

This website was designed and built to give Royal Park Clinic a professional online presence — helping patients in Ruwa and surrounding areas find the clinic, book appointments, and access key information easily from any device.

---

## ✅ Features

- Hero section with clinic name, tagline, and call-to-action buttons
- Full services page with 10 medical services
- Meet the team section with staff bios
- Online appointment booking form
- Embedded Google Map with directions
- Patient testimonials with star ratings
- Contact details and opening hours
- Floating WhatsApp chat button
- Sticky navigation bar
- SEO meta tags optimised for Ruwa, Zimbabwe
- Mobile-first design optimised for Android Chrome
